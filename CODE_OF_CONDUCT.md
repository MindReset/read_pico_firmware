# Code of Conduct

Keep discussions respectful, specific, and useful for people building or modifying Read Pico firmware. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, spam, and unrelated promotion are not welcome.

Maintainers may edit, close, hide, or remove disruptive content. Repeated abusive behavior may lead to blocking from the project. For sensitive conduct concerns, ask the maintainers for a private contact channel without posting personal details publicly.

# Code of Conduct

Keep discussions respectful, specific, and useful for people building or modifying Read Pico firmware. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, spam, and unrelated promotion are not welcome.

Maintainers may edit, close, hide, or remove disruptive content. Repeated abusive behavior may lead to blocking from the project. Report sensitive conduct concerns through the [official contact channels](https://dot.mindreset.tech/docs/contact); avoid posting private details publicly.
